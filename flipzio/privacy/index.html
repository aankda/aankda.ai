Privacy Policy

Local drafts first. Network only when you ask for generation.

This policy outlines how the Flipzio mobile apps (Android and iOS) manage saved drafts, generation requests, optional photo-assisted input, and background authentication. Published by AANKDA LLC.

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Local Storage

What Stays Local

Saved drafts and preferences stay inside the app's local database.

On-Demand Transit

When Data Leaves

Your item description and any attached photo are sent out over encrypted HTTPS only when you tap generate.

Form-Free

Silent Authentication

An anonymous session tied to the app installation authorizes requests without a visible sign-up.

On-Device Retention

Generated drafts and listing history stay inside the app's local database. Because Flipzio does not currently provide cloud sync, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app will permanently remove saved drafts, listing history, and local preferences stored on the device.

Secure Processing

The item description you enter, along with any photo you choose to attach, is sent to connected generation infrastructure only when you ask Flipzio to generate listing draft output, and those requests are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

Silent Setup

API access is controlled through an anonymous session tied to the app installation, avoiding any user-facing signup or login form.

Infrastructure Services

Flipzio currently uses Supabase for anonymous authentication session management and connected generation infrastructure for text-and-image-assisted draft processing.

Children's Privacy

Flipzio is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, the updated version will reflect a new effective date.

Privacy Contact

Questions about this policy or Flipzio's data handling can be sent to privacy@aankda.ai.
