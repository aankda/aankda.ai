Privacy Policy

Your listings stay private.

Saved drafts stay on-device. Your inputs and photos leave your device only when you request a listing generation.

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Local Storage

100% Local

Saved drafts, price estimates, and history stay inside the app's local database.

On-Demand Transit

On-Request Transit

Inputs and photos are sent over encrypted HTTPS only when you tap generate.

Privacy Built-In

No Account Setup

No login forms or email registrations. The app operates via anonymous session keys.

On-Device Retention

Because saved drafts and listing history are stored exclusively in the app's local database, clearing your app storage or uninstalling Flipzio will permanently remove your saved listings.

Secure Processing

When you request a generation, your item inputs are transmitted securely via HTTPS/TLS to our connected processing infrastructure strictly to generate your drafts. We do not store your inputs permanently.

Silent Session Setup

We use Supabase to manage anonymous session authorizations, allowing you to use draft features securely without providing personal signup information.

Children's Privacy

Flipzio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Privacy Contact

Questions about this policy or Flipzio's data handling can be sent to privacy@aankda.ai.
