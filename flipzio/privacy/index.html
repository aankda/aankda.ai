Privacy Policy

Local drafts stay on device until you ask us to generate.

This policy explains how Flipzio stores drafts locally and how text, photos, and anonymous requests are handled when you tap generate.

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Local Storage

Local storage

Saved drafts and preferences stay inside the app’s local database.

On-Demand Transit

When data leaves

Your item description and any attached photo leave the device only when you tap generate.

Form-Free

Anonymous sessions

An anonymous session tied to the app installation authorizes requests without a visible sign-up.

On-device retention

Generated drafts and history stay in the local database. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes saved drafts, history, and local preferences from that device.

Secure processing

The item description you enter, along with any photo you attach, is sent to generation infrastructure only when you ask Flipzio to create drafts.

Silent setup

API access is controlled through an anonymous session tied to the app installation, avoiding any user-facing signup or login form.

Infrastructure services

Flipzio uses Supabase for anonymous session management and connected draft generation.

Privacy Contact

Questions about this policy or Flipzio's data handling can be sent to privacy@aankda.ai. Flipzio is not intended for children under 13, and policy updates will be reflected here with a new effective date.
