Marketplace listing drafts, without the posting drag.

Start with a short description and, if helpful, a photo. Select your region to target the right marketplaces. Flipzio turns your inputs into platform-ready, region-aware listing drafts (including headline/title and base listing cards) for US marketplaces (Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, eBay, Mercari), optional local add-ons (Craigslist, Nextdoor), or South East Asia (Carousell).
