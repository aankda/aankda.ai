Marketplace listing drafts, without the posting drag.

Current capabilities

Built to accelerate listing prep, not automate posting.

Flipzio helps you get to cleaner draft copy faster, while keeping the seller in control of the final listing and marketplace handoff.

Photo-assisted drafts

Short text stays central, and photos help with visible details.

Optional photo input can support the draft when colors, labels, or visible wear are easier to show than describe.

Platform-style output

Draft bundles are tailored for supported marketplace styles.

Flipzio can shape copy for several marketplace-style outputs and include suggested price guidance without claiming exact valuation certainty.

Supported handoff paths

Different marketplaces currently continue in different ways.

eBay and Craigslist support a helper-style Copy & Open path. Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp currently stay draft-first and copy-first. Flipzio does not auto-post listings for you.
